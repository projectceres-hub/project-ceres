A. Rename Master Scenes to Campaign Scenes

Recommended. The right pane has Campaign Scenes as the main scene list, plus Sound Scenes for soundboard-only mixes. This makes the powerful cross-panel scene concept discoverable.

All scenes live in one list and carry a type badge. Very simple visually, but it blurs the difference between a local sound mix and a cross-panel campaign cue.

C. Keep Master Scenes Separate, Add Shortcuts

Lowest risk technically, but it preserves the discoverability problem. Users still have to learn the separate dock.

My recommendation is A: rename the concept to Campaign Scenes and make it the main right-pane scene surface.
